1. Hero Generation Guide

📜 How to get older-generation heroes (a two-generation lag)
  • Epic Recruitment: each time a new generation opens, Epic Recruitment unlocks heroes from two generations earlier. E.g. when Gen 4 opens → Epic Recruitment offers Gen 2 heroes; Gen 5 → Gen 3, and so on.
  • Hero's Path event: each generation's "Hero's Path" event lets you accumulate shards for one older hero:
    • Gen 4 → Hilde shards
    • Gen 5 → Jaeger shards
    • Gen 6 → Alcar shards
    • Gen 7 → Thrud shards

Gen 5 In-Depth Analysis (click to expand)

🛡️ Long Fei — Infantry｜Low-HP Lifesteal Tank
⚔️ Conquest Mode (Arena) A lifesteal tank that thrives on counterattacking from low HP. The three skills look contradictory in isolation, but the Signature Weapon ties them together perfectly.
  • Skill 1 — Ambrosia: 4s control immunity + attack speed up.
  • Skill 2: Reduces damage taken below 50% HP. Risk: getting one-shot by a single high-burst ultimate right as he crosses the 50% line.
  • Skill 3 — Mounted Mover: +48% Attack below 50% HP. Confusing on paper since his base Attack is lower than some Gen 3 infantry.
  • EWStrong Brew: Game-changing! Extends Skill 1 immunity by 1.5s and converts 15% of damage dealt into healing.
  • Skill synergy: Skill 1's attack speed → lifesteal frequency; below 50% HP, Skill 3's bonus Attack → lifesteal amount; combined with Skill 2's damage reduction, he's nearly unkillable while low + immune. A classic "very strong when winning, can't recover when losing" hero.
🛡️ Expedition Mode (Garrison & World Map) A very stable garrison hero.
  • EWDefensive: +15% garrison Attack.
  • Skill 1: 40% chance to reduce damage by 50%. Mathematically equivalent to "+50% Def or HP" in expected value. Useless for bear hunts.
  • Skill 2: +25% Defense (stable passive).
  • Skill 3: Low chance for 200% damage. Testing shows the proc rolls independently for Infantry, Cavalry, and Archer. This makes it hard to land peak burst on all three at once, but drastically lowers the floor risk of "never procced all battle" — excellent garrison stability.
🐎 Thrud — Cavalry｜Crowd Control + AoE Damage
⚔️ Conquest Mode (Arena) A devastating CC + AoE damage dealer in the arena.
  • Skill 1 (Ult) — Axe Flurry: 84% AoE (5 hits). Random targeting, so it bypasses the enemy front line and hits back-row heroes directly.
  • EWBerserker's Edge: Empowers the ult — 35% chance to add 1s stun, breaking the enemy's casting rhythm.
  • Skill 2 — Brutal Throw: 70% AoE + guaranteed 1.5s stun. Her base Attack is very high, so even single hits hurt.
  • Skill 3 — Feast of the Pack: Active buff that boosts the entire team's Attack (minions included). Usually fires around the 6s mark, right before the ult lands.
🛡️ Expedition Mode (Rally / World Map) Complex kit suited for mixed-troop PvP melees, very poor for bear hunts.
  • EWOffensive (only the 2nd offensive EW in the game): +15% rally Lethality (Wolf Kiss).
  • Skill 1 — Battle Hunger: +15% damage and -15% damage taken for Infantry & Archers. Effectively encourages you to bring less cavalry to maximize the buff — useful against pure 60/40 comps.
  • Skill 2 — Reckless Charge: 20% chance for cavalry to deal +100% extra damage. Testing shows when it procs, the attack hits Infantry, Cavalry, AND Archers simultaneously, chipping the enemy back lines before their infantry dies. But the damage scales with how much cavalry you bring — directly contradicts Skill 1.
  • Skill 3 — Ancestral Guidance: Every 4 cavalry attacks, whole army +25% damage / -25% damage taken (2 rounds). You miss the buff for the first two rounds and need surviving cavalry to stack it — relatively underwhelming.
🏹 Vivian — Archer｜Back-Row Sniper
⚔️ Conquest Mode (Arena) A high-burst back-row sniper.
  • Skill 1 (Ult) — Gilded Barrage: 252% AoE damage. The first skill that explicitly targets the enemy back row, also -50% attack speed to the back row (2s) — massively neuters enemy DPS.
  • Skill 2 — Lucky Strike: Single-target 140% damage, 50% chance to double. Paired with the highest base Attack in the game, the expected value is enormous.
  • Skill 3 — Priceless Shot: Multi-hit AoE (150% total). Not stated, but testing shows it can also reach the back row before the front collapses.
  • EWRefined Gunplay: Every time another skill fires, deals up to 70% damage to a random enemy (heroes prioritized). Reinforces her "decapitate the back row" identity.
🛡️ Expedition Mode (Rally / World Map) Needs all three troop types present and longer combat to shine.
  • EWDefensive: +15% garrison Defense.
  • Skill 1 — Crouching Tiger: Increases enemy damage taken. A rare and powerful multiplicative debuff — perfect for rally riders.
  • Skill 2 — Focus Fire: Every 4 attacks (shared counter across Inf/Cav/Arc), the next attack deals +100% damage. Usually lands on Inf's 2nd, Cav's 3rd, or Arc's 4th attack in round 1. But when the +100% triggers on the 4th attack, the 5th attack overwrites Skill 1's debuff, downgrading it to a weak 15% debuff — feels clunky.
  • Skill 3 — Trap of Greed: Every 4 archer attacks, next attack deals +60% extra damage. Testing shows the empowered archer attack hits Inf, Cav, AND Arc simultaneously (similar to Thrud's Skill 2). Excellent AoE in long PvP, but needs 4 rounds to warm up; if your comp lacks all three troop types (e.g. pure-infantry bear hunt), the value drops sharply.

🎯 Investment Recommendations

1. Conquest / Arena focus: Thrud and Vivian are must-builds — they introduce the new "ignore the front line, decapitate the back row" meta. Long Fei is a high-value pick for F2P / low-spender front-line durability.

2. Bear Hunt / PvE focus: Skip all three — save your resources! Their mechanics (forcing mixed troop ratios, multi-troop targeting, defense/survival oriented) don't fit a pure-infantry bear environment and underperform older heroes.

3. World Map PvP (Rally / Garrison): Garrison → Long Fei (stability). Rally leader → Thrud (multi-troop opening debuff). Rally rider → Vivian (strong damage-taken debuff, but needs a mixed Inf/Cav/Arc comp).

Gen 6 In-Depth Analysis (click to expand)

🏹 Yang — Archer｜RNG Burst Rally Leader
⚔️ Conquest Mode (Arena) Owns the highest archer base attack in the game (7,200). Pure-burst back-row sniper. The kit is RNG-heavy, but each layer multiplies.
  • Skill 1 (Ultimate) — Kin Throw: Linear AoE for 140% damage; the Frostkin deals the same damage again on its return path, effectively doubling the ult.
  • Skill 2 — Combo: 35% chance to add an extra hit. The more attacks Yang lands, the more value.
  • Skill 3 — Deadshot: Flat 15% Crit Rate — a clean multiplicative scalar on top of his already huge attack stat.
  • SigWFrostkin: Archer Lethality +133.5% / Archer HP +133.5%. Combined with the Quick Draw widget (56% damage to random enemy + 100% knockdown vs. Escorts), he can cripple the enemy's escort frontline.
  • Verdict: Even when behind, his raw stats keep him scoring; ahead, Combo + Crit + double-hit ult give single-round burst exceeding Vivian.
🛡️ Expedition Mode (Rally / World Map) Rosa's true successor and currently the strongest offensive rally leader. All three expedition skills are multiplicative damage boosts.
  • SigW Offensive: Rally Lethality +15% (Offensive Defense).
  • Skill 1 — Avalanche: Every 4 turns, all squads strike a target for +100% damage. Scales with battle length — a perfect fit for multi-turn Bear Hunt.
  • Skill 2 — Ice Zone: Yang's archers have a 40% chance to deal +100% damage on each hit — a flat +40% expected damage on your main DPS line.
  • Skill 3 — Ambush: Each turn, every squad has a 40% chance to deal increased damage (up to +40%). Applies to Infantry, Cavalry AND Archer — no troop-mix restriction unlike Thrud / Vivian.
  • Verdict: For Bear Hunt rallies, Yang as leader unshackles the entire comp: no troop-ratio requirement, no SigW dependency, and the longer the fight runs the stronger he gets.
🛡️ Triton — Infantry｜New Garrison Core
⚔️ Conquest Mode (Arena) Extremely beefy frontline tank, sustaining through invulnerability frames and self-buffs. Damage is modest, but survivability is in another league compared to Long Fei.
  • Skill 1 — Tidewill: 2s invulnerability followed by a forward dash dealing 140% AoE — perfect for dodging enemy ult windows.
  • Skill 2 — Regal Wrath: Self +24% Attack / +48% Defense for 4s. Classic "offense = defense" loop with Tidewill.
  • Skill 3 — Striking Hit: Adds 28% damage to basic attacks, compensating for his lower base attack.
  • SigWTidal Scepter: Infantry Lethality +133.5% / Infantry HP +133.5%.
  • Verdict: Top-tier arena frontliner. Against high-burst back rows (Vivian / Yang), those invulnerability frames are worth their weight in gold.
🛡️ Expedition Mode (Garrison / World Map) The new garrison ceiling: all three expedition skills are team-wide multiplicative buffs.
  • SigW Defensive: Garrison Defense +15% (Whale Call).
  • Skill 1 — Command of Power: All squads +25% Defense. Pure passive — full uptime.
  • Skill 2 — Warfare of Power: All squads +30% skill damage. Effective for both Bear Hunt and garrison — a very rare multiplicative "skill damage" buff.
  • Skill 3 — Oath of Power: Infantry HP +20%, Cavalry / Archer HP +30%. The back-row archers get the bigger HP boost, plugging the weakest point of a 60/20/20 garrison comp.
  • Verdict: Bumps Long Fei to second-pick status as the top garrison infantry. F2P players normally don't lead garrisons (that's R5/R4/spender territory), so Triton's F2P value is mainly: defending your own city when attacked, and contributing multiplicative buffs when joining an alliance garrison.
🐎 Sophia — Cavalry｜Mixed-Comp PvP Controller
⚔️ Conquest Mode (Arena) A "ramp-up" cavalry hero — weak in the early seconds, strong once marks stack.
  • Skill 1 — Puppet Master: 140% AoE + 1s Confuse. The confuse cuts off enemy cast sequences.
  • Skill 2 — Scalding Mark: 70% damage + applies a 4s mark increasing target's damage taken by 6%.
  • Skill 3 — Scalding Mark – Lash: Against marked targets, Sophia gains +24% Attack and +12% damage.
  • SigWScarlet Rose: Cavalry Lethality +133.5% / Cavalry HP +133.5%.
  • Verdict: The combo is tightly chained but needs time to set up. Short-window burst is well below Petra / Yang; advantage grows against high-HP tanks.
🛡️ Expedition Mode (Rally / World Map) A unique "conditional-burst" cavalry leader, suited to multi-troop PvP, NOT Bear Hunt.
  • SigW Defensive: Garrison Lethality +15% (Queen of Night).
  • Skill 1 — Arcane Pact: 40% chance each turn for all squads to take 50% less damage. A very strong random-DR buff, the multiplicative garrison equivalent of Long Fei's main skill.
  • Skill 2 — Terror – Deathblow: Applies Terror; the following turn, cavalry deals +200% damage to Terrified targets.
  • Skill 3 — Terror – Annihilation: All squads deal +75% damage to Terror targets. Chains with Skill 2 — every Terror proc empowers the whole team.
  • Verdict: A pure-infantry Bear Hunt comp can't exploit the Terror chain; but in three-troop World-Map PvP and Alliance Duel, Sophia as leader can instantly delete a key target.

🎯 Gen 6 Investment Plan

1. Bear Hunt / Rally focus: Must-build Yang. Avalanche + Ice Zone + Ambush stack multiplicatively across all troop types and only get stronger over time — the best rally-leader investment since Rosa.

2. Garrison / World-Map Defense: Triton is the new top infantry pick. "+25% Def / +30% skill damage / +30% Cav-Arc HP" are all multiplicative — clearly ahead of Long Fei. F2P players normally don't lead garrisons, so Triton's main value is defending your own city and contributing to alliance garrisons.

3. Alliance Duel PvP: Consider Sophia if resources permit. The Terror chain shreds enemy rally leaders in mixed-troop fights, but is dead weight in a pure-infantry Bear Hunt setup.

4. F2P guidance (acquisition channels):

  • Yang (event hero — NOT roulette): Obtained from Daily Deals / Hero Rally / Strongest Governor / Kingdom of Power events. F2P should funnel all Universal Hero Shards into Yang — gems don't help here.
  • Sophia (Gen 6 roulette cavalry): Requires gems. But F2P already has Petra covering the cavalry slot — don't burn gems on Sophia; save them for Gen 7's Wee & Woo (the Gen 7 roulette archer).
  • Triton (Hall of Heroes / Swordland Showdown Shop): Slow F2P pickup; consider only with monthly pass / light spend.
  • Keep Long Fei (Gen 5 roulette) for infantry and Petra (Gen 3 roulette) for cavalry.

Gen 7 In-Depth Analysis (click to expand)

🏹 Wee & Woo — Archer｜The New DPS King
⚔️ Conquest Mode (Arena) Highest base attack in the entire game (8,656) — pure DPS, no compromises.
  • Skill 1 (Ultimate) — High Ground: 420% AoE. The highest single-skill multiplier in the game — one cast deletes a low-HP back row.
  • Skill 2 — Incendiaries: 84% initial hit + 2s burn (25% every 0.5s), ~184% total damage.
  • Skill 3 — Frontal Support: Self +26% Attack — directly amplifies an already chart-topping attack stat.
  • SigWMortar: Archer Lethality +133.5% / Archer HP +133.5%. With the "Zero Sympathy" widget (+14% Attack Speed for 5s) the burst window goes nuclear.
  • Verdict: Replaces Vivian and Yang as the new back-row sniper. HP pool is the lowest of the three Gen 7 heroes, so a strong front line (Charles / Triton) is required to unlock his ceiling.
🛡️ Expedition Mode (Rally / World Map) The three expedition skills are pure multiplicative damage, but the SigW widget is garrison-only, so as a rally leader he actually loses out to Yang. Best deployed as a rally rider.
  • SigW Defensive: Garrison +15% Attack (Landmines). Important: this widget does NOT activate when leading a rally — Wee & Woo's biggest weakness as a leader. By comparison, Yang's "Rally Lethality +15%" widget is a direct multiplicative gain for the entire rally.
  • Skill 1 — Artillerymen: Squad +15% Attack and +10% Lethality. Pure dual passive — and since riders only contribute their first expedition skill, this is the strongest rider skill in the game. He's worth considering for the Bear Hunt rider slot: compared to Hilde's Attack + Defense, Wee & Woo swaps Defense for Lethality — and for a pure-DPS Bear Hunt, Lethality > Defense, the more offense-leaning pick.
  • Skill 2 — Chain Shelling: Squad deals +30% to Archer, +25% to Infantry. Useful in World-Map PvP; Bear (Infantry type) gets the +25%, but a pure-infantry Bear Hunt only triggers this single skill.
  • Skill 3 — Boom Boom: 50% chance for squad attacks to deal +50% damage (+25% expected).
  • Verdict:
    • Leader comparison: Yang's rally lethality multiplier is about (1+1.335)×(1+0.15) ≈ 2.69×; Wee & Woo's is about (1+1.605) ≈ 2.60×. Add Yang's Avalanche scaling in long fights, and Yang remains the F2P rally-leader pick.
    • Best role: Sit him in rally rider slot #1 for the team-wide +15% Attack / +10% Lethality, or replace Yang only after Wee & Woo is fully maxed and your raw stats clearly exceed Yang.
🛡️ Charles — Infantry｜Mech-Detonation Tank
⚔️ Conquest Mode (Arena) A fusion tank with built-in second-wave burst — more offensive-tank flavored than Triton.
  • Skill 1 — Vengeance: 140% AoE + 1s stun + self +100% Attack for 2s. Direct ult-interrupt.
  • Skill 2 — Parting Gift: When his mech (escort vehicle) is destroyed, detonates a 280% AoE — punishes focus-fire.
  • Skill 3 — Nimble Soul: 50% chance to take 50% less damage. Expected DR 25%, matching Long Fei but more reliable.
  • SigWJustice Fist: Infantry Lethality +133.5% / Infantry HP +133.5%. The "Mechanic" widget restores 35% HP + 30% Defense once when dropping below 50% HP.
  • Verdict: A frontline that eats damage AND retaliates. Survives high-burst DPS like Wee & Woo comfortably.
🛡️ Expedition Mode (Garrison / World Map) Replaces Triton as the new garrison core. All three expedition skills are pure team buffs.
  • SigW Defensive: Garrison HP +15% (Shock and Awe).
  • Skill 1 — Intimidation: Enemy Lethality -20% — directly suppresses incoming damage.
  • Skill 2 — Iron Bodies: Squad takes -20% damage.
  • Skill 3 — Great Justice: Squad HP +25%.
  • Verdict: Combined, that's "enemy DMG ×0.8, our damage taken ×0.8, our HP ×1.25" — effective HP ×1.95. The new garrison infantry king, replacing Triton. F2P players normally don't lead garrisons, so Charles' main value is defending your own city when attacked and contributing multiplicative buffs to alliance garrisons.
🐎 Ava — Cavalry｜Team-Wide Armor Breaker
⚔️ Conquest Mode (Arena) A "set-up" cavalry centered on area DoT and vulnerability marks.
  • Skill 1 — Color Bomb: 3s area DoT, 70% damage every 0.5s (~420% total). Same total as Wee & Woo's ult, but spread out — hard to one-shot a single target.
  • Skill 2 — Corruption Bomb: Damage + 25% increased damage taken for 2s. The core synergy is to feed Wee & Woo's ult under this debuff.
  • Skill 3 — Disguise Pigment: Self +75% Defense — survives counter-burst.
  • SigWChameleos: Cavalry Lethality +160.5% / Cavalry HP +160.5%.
  • Verdict: A pure "secondary DPS / opener" — best as Wee & Woo's amp partner, much weaker solo.
🛡️ Expedition Mode (Rally / World Map) The first hero with an "enemy Defense -25%" expedition skill. The only Gen 6/7 rally leader where all four expedition abilities (widget + 3 skills) are universal — equally strong as a leader or as a rider.
  • SigW Offensive: Rally Lethality +15% (Color Storm). Applies to all troop types — even the 80% archer portion of a Bear Hunt rally benefits.
  • Skill 1 — Dissolution: Enemy squads -25% Defense (multiplicative debuff, the only one in the game). Devastating against Bear, and works as either leader or rider (riders only contribute their first expedition skill — Ava is a tier-S rider candidate).
  • Skill 2 — Chiaroscuro: Every 4 turns, all enemies take +50% damage (universal; leader-only).
  • Skill 3 — Light and Cold: Squad Lethality +25% (universal; leader-only).
  • Note: The Chameleos SigW's base "Cavalry Lethality +160.5%" only boosts the 10% cavalry portion of a Bear Hunt rally — this is the one piece of value she "wastes" as Bear leader, and the main reason Yang still edges her out (Yang's archer SigW + Ice Zone align with the 80% archers).
  • Verdict:
    • Optimal stack: Yang leader + Ava rider slot #1 — the rally enjoys Yang's archer SigW + Ice Zone AND Ava's Dissolution -25% Defense simultaneously.
    • Ava is also a viable Bear Hunt leader and a direct replacement for Petra in your lineup: Widget +15% Lethality + Dissolution -25% Def + Chiaroscuro +50% every 4 turns + Light and Cold +25% Lethality — four universal multipliers. If your Yang isn't fully built yet or your alliance runs parallel rallies, Ava leading the second rally is completely fine. Field-tested: Ava + Yang shifts the entire bear-damage distribution clearly above Petra (Gen-3) + Yang, with the median running ~30% higher; the only trade-off is that when Petra's two signature-weapon skills both proc, her p99 single-hit peak edges out Ava's — meaning what Ava buys you is steadier, higher average output.
    • For mixed-comp World-Map PvP rallies, the cavalry ratio is higher and Ava's cavalry SigW finally pulls its weight — there she's an even stronger leader pick.

🎯 Gen 7 Investment Plan

1. Bear Hunt / Rally (leader = still Yang): Gen 7 does not produce a better rally leader than Yang. Yang's widget grants "+15% Rally Lethality" while Wee & Woo's widget is "+15% Defender Attack" — that gap alone keeps Yang at #1. The real Gen 7 upgrade for Bear Hunt is adding Ava as a rally rider: her first skill "Dissolution" gives the whole rally -25% enemy Defense (multiplicative), which raises every alliance member's Bear damage a full tier.

2. Bear Hunt / Rally (rider slot #1): Build Wee & Woo. Since riders only contribute their first expedition skill, Artillerymen (+15% Attack / +10% Lethality team-wide) is the strongest rider-skill in the game, and is worth considering for the Bear Hunt rider slot — same dual-stat buff as Hilde, but Lethality instead of Defense, which is exactly what a pure-DPS Bear rally wants. Higher ROI than building him as a leader.

3. Garrison focus: Charles is the new garrison infantry king. "-20% enemy Lethality / -20% damage taken / +25% HP" multiplies to near-double effective HP. F2P players normally don't lead garrisons, so Charles' main value is defending your own city and contributing buffs to alliance garrisons.

4. Alliance Duel / Arena: Wee & Woo + Charles + Ava is the meta trio: Charles tanks, Wee & Woo deletes the board (420% AoE + highest base attack in the game), Ava sets up vulnerability and armor break.

5. F2P guidance (split your channels):

  • Universal Hero Shards → Yang (rally leader remains the top pick; Yang is NOT a roulette hero, so the only F2P path to star him up is shards + event participation).
  • Gems → Wee & Woo (the Gen 7 Hero Roulette archer — the only archer you can actually pull with gems, rider slot #1 / new arena king). Start saving gems from Gen 6 onward and skip Gen 6's Sophia (cavalry slot already covered by Petra).
  • Charles (event hero, same channel as Yang): Second priority for Universal Hero Shards (garrison core).
  • Ava (Hall of Heroes / Swordland Showdown Shop): Slow F2P pickup, consider with monthly pass / Hero Recruit. But her Dissolution rider value is so high that long-term shard accumulation is worth it.

Bear Rally Key Mechanics

  • All three expedition skills of the rally leader activate
  • The entire rally's base Attack and Lethality are determined by the leader's stats. The leader's gear, research, and hero stars determine how hard the rally hits.
  • Only the top 4 riders by skill level contribute their "1st Expedition Skill." The leader should verify that riders' first heroes are set correctly. Riders' own gear, research, and attack panel are irrelevant — inside a rally, the leader's panel applies.
  • That's why Chenko's 1st expedition skill (+25% Lethality) should be maxed first
  • Gear only applies when you are the rally leader — your gear has no effect when joining someone else's rally
  • Sig Weapons give multiplicative bonuses, gear gives additive bonuses → Sig Weapons become more valuable late game
  • Don't send a single troop type. Damage does not scale linearly with troop count (diminishing returns). If 10,000 archers deal 1M damage, 40,000 archers (4×) deal only $\sqrt{4} = 2$× = 2M damage.
  • Base damage weight by troop type: Infantry ×$\frac{1}{3}$, Cavalry ×$1$, Archer ×$\frac{4}{3}$. This means archers generate the highest base damage per unit of troop capacity. → Max archers first, minimal infantry, cavalry fills the gap.

9. Common Mistakes (Must Avoid)

  1. Spreading hero investment too thin — stick to 3 core heroes; max each one before moving to the next
  2. Evenly distributing skill points — concentrate on one skill branch
  3. Leaving builders idle — every idle second is wasted
  4. Skipping events — Hall of Governors and similar events have huge rewards; save speed-ups and resources for event periods
  5. Ignoring the Mystery Shop — multiple daily refreshes offer free items
  6. Not joining a strong alliance early — join an active alliance within the first 2 days
  7. Spending gems too early — gems are only for the Hero Roulette
  8. Using Emergency Mobilization and Rush simultaneously — they don't stack; using both at once wastes one
  9. Neglecting City Hall upgrades — City Hall level caps all other buildings
  10. Ignoring blue-tier heroes — blue heroes still have value for gathering
  11. Maxing old hero Sig Weapons over investing in new heroes — don't abandon already-invested heroes too quickly
  12. F2P tip: prioritize Lord Gear, Lord Gems, and Pet Development over hero Sig Weapons. New heroes release every ~2 months and their base stats will outclass older generations, leading to exhausting constant hero swaps.
  13. War Academy exchange — use only Coins to exchange for Gold Dust, never Gold. Gold is a critical upgrade resource; Coins are far less valuable to us.
